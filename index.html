What are NFTs?

NFTs (Non-Fungible Tokens) are unique digital assets that represent ownership of a specific item or piece of content, such as art, music, videos, or collectibles. Unlike cryptocurrencies like Bitcoin, NFTs are one-of-a-kind and cannot be exchanged on a one-to-one basis. They are stored on blockchain technology, ensuring their authenticity and ownership.
